About this analysis

Why this dashboard exists

Medical device recalls can affect millions of patients, yet the public record is split across device, event and manufacturer files. This dashboard joins them to show where recalls concentrate by manufacturer, device, risk class, country and distribution scope, and to separate all reported recalls from those linked to a cleaned device record.

Analysis type
Descriptive, exploratory (EDA) analysis of public recalls, 1997–2018. No prediction or causal claims.
Audience
Patient-safety and regulatory analysts, data and quality teams, students, journalists and the public.
Built on
ICIJ Faulty Medical Devices Global Dataset, processed by a reproducible Python pipeline with 13 automated checks.

Methodology & principles

How the data was analyzed, cleaned and organized

Analysis

  • Business questions were defined before cleaning; every KPI and chart is computed from the cleaned tables, never hard-coded.
  • Free text is normalized explicitly: device names lose the “Device Recall” prefix and distributed_to becomes four distribution scopes.
  • Charts state their denominator — all recalls or the device-linked subset — and fold long tails into “Other (grouped)”.

Cleaning

  • Rules live in code: complete-case devices, events with at least 15 non-null values, and columns above 40% nulls dropped.
  • Keys are checked for uniqueness, labels are unified, dates parsed, and duplicate or constant columns removed.
  • 13 automated checks verify keys, completeness, allowed values, joins and report consistency.

Organization

  • Raw data stays immutable; data/processed/ and this dashboard are regenerated by the pipeline.
  • One command rebuilds everything: python -m src.pipeline (--skip-etl reuses the enriched CSV).
  • Three static files, no database or server; the notebooks document the exploration and decisions.

Limitations: the complete-case device filter biases device-linked results toward US FDA records; 973 recalls have no parsable date; distribution scopes come from rule-based normalization of free text, not a gazetteer.

Source: ICIJ Faulty Medical Devices Global Dataset (Kaggle). Static dashboard generated by python -m src.pipeline.
